Applications of Electronics

Electronics is everywhere – from your mobile phone to renewable energy systems.

Power Systems

Power electronics control conversion and flow of electrical energy in grids, inverters, and motor drives.

Embedded Systems

Microcontrollers control devices like washing machines, cars, and IoT gadgets using programmed logic.

IoT & Robotics

Sensors, actuators, and controllers work together to build smart devices, robots, and automation systems.

Communication

Electronics enables communication systems like mobile networks, Wi-Fi, satellite links, and optical fiber.
