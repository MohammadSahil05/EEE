Basic Electronic Components

Get familiar with the building blocks of every electronic circuit.

Resistor

A resistor limits the flow of electric current in a circuit. It is used to control voltage and current levels.

  • Symbol: Zig-zag or rectangle
  • Unit: Ohm (Ω)
  • Use: Current limiting, voltage divider

Capacitor

A capacitor stores electrical energy in the form of an electric field and releases it when required.

  • Symbol: Two parallel plates
  • Unit: Farad (F)
  • Use: Filtering, timing, coupling

Diode

A diode allows current to flow only in one direction. It acts like an electrical one-way valve.

  • Symbol: Triangle with a line
  • Use: Rectification, protection

Transistor

A transistor works as a switch or amplifier for electronic signals. It’s the heart of modern electronics.

  • Types: NPN, PNP, MOSFET
  • Use: Switching, amplification

Integrated Circuit (IC)

An IC is a complete mini-circuit built on a small chip containing many transistors, resistors, etc.

  • Use: Microcontrollers, op-amps, timers
